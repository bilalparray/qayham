Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

WhatsApp Gallery · Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy & Terms of Use explains how WhatsApp Gallery (the “App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), developed by QAYHAM, handles information and how you may use the App. WhatsApp Gallery is an Android application that lets you browse, preview, organize, share, save, and manage photos, videos, and documents already stored on your device, including WhatsApp media that is accessible to the App.

The short version: WhatsApp Gallery is designed around on-device media access. We do not operate a server that stores your WhatsApp photos or videos as part of the App's core gallery functionality. Your media remains on your device unless you explicitly choose an action that sends, saves, shares, or otherwise makes a file available to another app or service.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on our external servers as part of the App's core media-browsing functionality. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location through the App's core functionality.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, documents, or other WhatsApp media to our servers.
  • We do not use your media to build a personal profile.
  • We do not sell your personal information or media.

If a future version of the App introduces a feature that changes what information is collected or shared, this policy will be updated as required and the relevant disclosures will be provided.

2. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

The App requests Android permissions only as necessary to provide its features:

  • Photos & Videos / Storage access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, and, where applicable, All files access / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): used to locate and read WhatsApp media that is accessible on your device so the App can display, preview, and manage it. Access may also be used to save copies to your gallery when you explicitly request that action.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → WhatsApp Gallery → Permissions. Revoking access may limit the App's ability to show or manage your media.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To provide faster browsing, the App stores certain information locally on your device only. This may include:

  • Media index & thumbnails: local references to media and generated thumbnails used to make browsing faster.
  • App settings: preferences such as theme, grid size, and other local settings.
  • Purchase entitlement information: a local record used to determine whether an applicable paid feature has been unlocked.

This locally stored information is not intended to be transmitted to QAYHAM's servers.

Clearing App data or uninstalling the App may remove locally stored index, cache, and settings data. Clearing the media index or thumbnail cache does not restore media files that have already been deleted.

Media deleted externally, including by WhatsApp, Android, another application, a file manager, or the user, may become unavailable to WhatsApp Gallery. The App cannot guarantee recovery of media that is no longer present or accessible on the device.
